**Effective July 2, 2026 by Red Pill, Blue Pill Studios, LLC**

This policy explains how Red Pill Blue Pill Studios ("we," "us," "our") handles privacy on this website, **redpillbluepillstudios.com**. Our individual apps each have their own privacy policy covering that app; this policy covers the website itself.

## The short version

This website does not collect your personal data. It is a static site with no user accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies set by us. We do not build profiles of visitors or sell any information.

## Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information through this website. There are no forms, sign-ups, or accounts. If you email us using a link on the site, we receive whatever you choose to include in that email (such as your email address and message) solely so we can reply.

## Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like most web hosts, GitHub's servers may automatically record standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in security and operational logs when pages are requested. That processing is handled by GitHub under its own privacy practices; we do not control it and do not receive that data. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

## Fonts and third parties

Fonts used on this site are self-hosted — your browser loads them from this site, not from a third-party font service. We do not embed third-party advertising, analytics, or social tracking scripts.

## Cookies

We do not set cookies on this website.

## Our apps

Each app we publish has its own privacy policy describing how that app handles data. For example, see the [Sagitta Chains privacy policy](/apps/sagitta-chains/privacy). Those policies apply to the apps themselves and are separate from this website policy.

## Children's privacy

This website does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). Because we collect no personal information through the site, none is gathered from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will appear on this page before taking effect.
